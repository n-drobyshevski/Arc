Behaviour

What each key does in arc

Over MIDI, arc only hears notes, the clock and a few SysEx messages. It can't tell when SOUND or FX is pressed on the device, so those keys don't pretend to light up. A tap on one of them opens its shortcuts from the guide instead.

Acts in arc

  • Pads: hold to hear the sample in the browser. Several fingers play a chord.
  • A–D: pick the group the pads play. The navy key is the group shown.
  • KEYS: switch between PADS and KEYS. In KEYS the pads become notes of the last sound.
  • RECORD: arm REC. Recording starts with the first sound, and a second tap stops and saves the take.
  • − / +: octave down or up in KEYS, previous or next group in PADS. Their LEDs show which way can still move.

Lights from the EP-133

  • Pads glow orange with velocity and fade over 300 ms, the same as the grid.
  • Group LEDs (the dots above A–D) light for the group shown and flicker with the groups that sound. Follow moves the selection.
  • PLAY brightens while the device plays, from MIDI clock start and stop.
  • Display: BPM in the seven-segment digits, ▶ while playing, ● for REC (blinking when armed), the A–D boxes, the four-colour ring (one quarter per group), the piano icon in KEYS, and the metronome ticking on the beat.
  • Plate: where the unit prints its name, arc writes the project and connection (orange line) and the last hit (black line).

Shows its shortcuts

  • The two-tone keys (SOUND/EDIT, MAIN/COMMIT, TEMPO/LOOP, SAMPLE/CHOP, TIMING/CORRECT, FX/OUTPUT, ERASE/SYSTEM), SHIFT, FADER, the X / Y knobs and the fader open a card with their combos from the shortcut guide.
  • VOLUME explains that arc's sound follows the computer's volume.
  • The printed LEDs next to LEVEL, PITCH, LPF and the other fader labels stay dark: arc can't see the fader's assignment.
  • The card links to the full guide, already filtered to that key.
  • Nothing is ever sent to the device. arc only reads in Live.

Keyboard: the numpad plays the pads as laid out on the device (7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3 / . 0 Enter). Laptops without one use four QWERTY rows: 1 2 3 / Q W E / A S D / Z X C. Holding a key holds the gate. F1–F4 pick A–D, K toggles KEYS, R arms or stops REC, and − / + do what the device's −/+ do. The guide overlay lists them. Try them on the device above.

After sign-off

Build order

The repo rule still holds: Kotlin core/ first, then the web port, which carries the "Port of …" header. Each step ships on its own and passes npm run check.

REC engine

Port TakeRecorder.kt to web/src/core/features/takeRecorder.ts, with the same tests. Tap the mix in MixerHost.render (platform/audio/liveMixer.ts), so a take holds exactly what the browser played. Cap a take at 10 minutes, as Android does.

Takes storage and actions

Add an IndexedDB takes store in platform/storage/db.ts, kept apart from arc-demo. Also copy takes into the library folder when one is picked (platform/storage/external.ts), as Android keeps them in Documents/arc. Wire Play/Stop, Save WAV (files/save), Share (only where Web Share takes files), and Delete with the inline confirmation. To EP-133 opens UploadSheet with the WAV.

REC chip and Takes UI on the existing grid

Copy the REC and take strings from MirrorText.kt into mirrorText.ts. Add the chip to the display strip and the panel, and a Takes section in Live tools. Add Playwright steps for arm, record, stop and the new row.

Phone grid in the K.O. II's colours

Restyle the one-group grid with black pads, light A–D keys with their icons and LEDs, and an orange RECORD key next to D. The layout and touch behaviour stay the same, and the PO-style grid stays on Android unless you want it there too.

Device view

New web/src/ui/live/DeviceView.tsx. It reuses glow.ts, the press.ts gate and chord logic, the KEYS note maths in keys.ts, and the Knob and Fader visuals from GuideKeys.tsx. MirrorScreen.tsx picks the view at 900 px or wider. Add the keyboard map (numpad, QWERTY rows, F1–F4, K, R, −/+), and −/+ stepping through groups in PADS.

Shortcut cards

Filter guideData.ts by key name for the card, and add a "show all" link to #/guide with the filter applied. Add the new tags to the guide overlay.

Docked tools at 1200 px

SideZone becomes a docked column at that width. Back no longer closes it there.

Clear the "Check" items

Clear the open questions from the audit: the first-run overlay, the toast lifetime, the Settings storage line, the project tile sizes, and the pads sheet rows.
